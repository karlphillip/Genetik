# Rank-aware LM fallback

## Policy

`ConstantFitConfig::rankAwareFallback` is an opt-in policy for ordinary scalar
Levenberg–Marquardt fitting. It runs ordinary LM first, then invokes the existing
rank-aware selected-constant fitter at most once only if ordinary LM stops with
`LMStop::SingularSystem`, the valid result is unchanged, and the remaining local
and shared objective allowance can fund the retry baseline, selector derivative
rows, and one LM objective evaluation. Full-rank fits, other native stops,
ordinary improvements, and insufficient allowances do not start a retry.

The retry reduces its LM mathematical-operation cap by the ordinary phase's
spent arithmetic. Selector evaluations and retry objective calls consume the
remaining per-fit and search-wide allowances. A failed, non-improving, or
exhausted retry keeps the best valid ordinary result. The production default is
unchanged. `rankAware=true` remains the separate always-rank-aware option.
Parallel symbolic search and checkpointed `ScalarMathematicsRun` reject this
mode, matching the existing rank-aware restrictions and preserving checkpoint
payloads.

For each `ConstantFitResult`, `ordinaryOptimizerStop` records the first phase;
`rankAwareFallbackAttempted`, `rankAwareFallbackOutcome`,
`rankAwareFallbackOptimizerStop`, and the `ordinaryPhase*` / `fallbackPhase*`
fields report phase decisions and work. `SymbolicRegressionResult` aggregates
singular stops, attempts, improved fits, objective calls, selector work and LM
work in `rankAwareFallbackWork`.

## Deterministic contract results

The public direct-fit fixture uses `(0.5+0.5)*x+0` against `2*x+3` on seven
training rows. Ordinary LM stops at `singular_system` with valid unchanged MSE
13. Its one loss call is followed by one rank-aware retry: the selected
postfix directions recover the identifiable coefficient sum and intercept, the
public training loss independently recomputes near zero, and held-out MSE on
`x={-0.5,2.5,4}` is `1.47254e-29`. The fallback selects postfix constant nodes
`[0,5]`, stops at native `loss_tolerance`, and uses 21 selector evaluations.
Ordinary fitting uses one objective call / 98 LM arithmetic operations; the
retry uses nine calls / 448 LM arithmetic operations. Tests also confirm:

- A full-rank `a*x+c` fit never invokes fallback.
- An ordinary `EvaluationLimit` stop never invokes fallback.
- A singular fit with fewer than the required retry units preserves its valid
  MSE-13 incumbent.
- A retry that exhausts its arithmetic cap preserves the valid ordinary
  incumbent and reconciles objective, selector and arithmetic counters.
- Ordinary symbolic search invokes the fallback through the public search API;
  seed 81723 evaluates eight candidates, sees three ordinary singular stops,
  makes three retries and improves three fits. It charges 20 ordinary fit calls,
  27 retry calls, 63 selector evaluations, and 392 / 1,344 LM arithmetic
  operations in the two phases. The winner has training MSE `3.9443e-30`.
  This deterministic path contract does not compare an ordinary-only search
  winner or score held-out rows.
- The installed-package consumer compiles and exercises direct fallback fitting.

The focused Release and ASan/UBSan fitting contracts pass. The full Release
CTest run passed 140/143 tests. The unrelated research-program test exceeded its
60-second CTest timeout; the full installed-package fixture exceeded its
300-second inner consumer-build timeout on this ARM host; and hygiene found
Python bytecode created by the preflight command. The generated bytecode was
removed and the hygiene check then passed. A standalone installed CMake
consumer linked against `Genetik::Genetik` and passed the direct fallback test.

`x^2-0.5*x+1.25` tasks; eight paired seeds 91001–91008; identical one-core
searches with Add/Multiply grammar, depth 2/2, 50 candidate evaluations,
25,000 search-wide training-loss calls, LM bounds `[-8,8]`, and identical

matrix is recorded in
[`research/rank_aware_lm_fallback_20261128/admission/protocol.json`](../research/rank_aware_lm_fallback_20261128/admission/protocol.json).

No target search was started. The required synthetic pipeline preflight
refused before any runner, verifier, scorer or aggregator stage executed:
`preflight spec is not bound to this exact campaign protocol`. The refusal,
exact command, spec, protocol, adapter and hashes are preserved under
`research/rank_aware_lm_fallback_20261128/admission/`. Because admission did
not pass, the target comparison has no outcomes and cannot support a
recommendation about final-winner recovery or computational cost. Do not infer
search benefit from the deterministic single-candidate fit or search-path
contract.
